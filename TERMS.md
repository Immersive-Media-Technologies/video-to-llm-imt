# Documents made with V2L-IMT and their web addresses

V2L-IMT is a tool that runs on your computer. Among other things it can turn a video into a written
document — a guide or notes illustrated with frames of that video — and, if you ask, give that document a
web address. This page says who is responsible for what when that happens. It is written in plain words;
it is not legal advice, and the law of your country applies to you as it would without this page.

## What the tool does

- `video2llm guide` writes the document **on your computer**, into `~/Documents/V2L-IMT/<title>/`
  (or the folder you chose). Nothing is sent anywhere.
- `video2llm link` uploads that folder to **a site of your own** — your Neocities site, or with
  `--github` your GitHub Pages — using the account you signed up for yourself and the key or sign-in
  you gave the tool. The key is kept on your computer (`~/.config/video2llm/`) and is used for nothing
  else. The resulting address is yours: nobody else is told about it, it is not listed anywhere by the
  tool, and the page itself asks search engines not to index it (`noindex`).

## What Immersive Media Technologies does not do

- We do not host the documents, do not store copies of them, do not see the key, the account or the
  address, and do not receive any data about who made what. There is no server of ours in this flow.
- We do not review, moderate or endorse the content of any document. The text is written by the model
  you run; the frames come from the video you gave it.
- We cannot remove, change or take down a document — only the owner of the account it sits on can
  (delete the folder on the site, or the site, or the account).

## What you are responsible for

- **The video.** Make sure you may use its frames in a document: your own footage, material you have
  permission for, or a use that is allowed where you live (quoting a few frames of a tutorial in notes
  about it is usually fine; republishing someone's film as screenshots is not).
- **The content.** The document is yours, published under your account, under the site's own terms
  ([Neocities](https://neocities.org/legal/terms), [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy)). Those
  terms, and the law, decide what may be put there — not this page.
- **The address.** Anyone who has the address can open the page. Share it only with people you mean to.

## A line on every page

Every document carries «Made with V2L-IMT» and a link to this page in its footer, so that a reader knows
what made it and what that means. The attribution required by the [license](LICENSE) is this line.
